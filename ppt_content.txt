WHAT IS DISCOVERY ENGINE SAYS? 

The wishlist captures desire, not commitment.Saving an item can mean high intent, comparison, future use, price monitoring, or inspiration.
The friction shifts from discovery to confidence.Fit, fabric and real-world product uncertainty emerge as key questions before users commit.
The opportunity is to close the confidence gap at the wishlist → decision moment.Help users answer “Is this right for me?” before they postpone the purchase.
